Facebook Page Kaise Banaye 2026 — A to Z Complete Hindi Guide

📅 June 2026  •  ⏱️ 12 min read  •  ✍️ TechPaisa Team

Dosto Facebook par 300 crore se zyada users hain — yeh duniya ka sabse bada social media platform hai! Agar aap apna business grow karna chahte hain, paise kamana chahte hain ya apna brand banana chahte hain — toh Facebook Page banana zaroori hai. Aaj main aapko A to Z complete guide dunga!

Facebook Page Se ₹10,000-50,000 Mahine!

Lakhs Indians sirf Facebook Page se paise kama rahe hain — aap bhi kar sakte hain!

📌 Facebook Page Kya Hota Hai?

Facebook Page ek public profile hota hai jo business, brand, celebrity ya organization ke liye banaya jaata hai. Personal profile se alag — Page par unlimited followers ho sakte hain aur aap apna business promote kar sakte hain. Page bilkul free mein banta hai!

💡 Pro Tip: Personal profile aur Page alag hote hain — Page se business karo, personal profile personal rakho!

📱 Facebook Page Kaise Banaye — Step by Step

1
Facebook App Kholo aur Login Karo
Apne phone mein Facebook app kholo. Apne personal account se login karo. Facebook Page banana ke liye pehle personal account hona zaroori hai — agar nahi hai toh pehle banao.
2
Pages Section Mein Jao
App ke neeche ya upar menu mein "Pages" option dhundho. Agar directly nahi milta toh search bar mein "Pages" likho. "Create" ya "New Page" button par click karo.
3
Page Ka Naam Rakho
Page ka naam bahut sochke rakho — yeh aapka brand hai! Naam mein aapka topic hona chahiye jaise "TechPaisa Tips", "Health Guru Hindi", "Daily News India" — yaadgar aur simple naam best hota hai.
4
Category Select Karo
Aapke page ka topic kya hai uske hisaab se category chunno jaise — Blog, Entertainment, Education, Health/Beauty, Business — sahi category se zyada log dhundhenge aapka page!
5
Profile Photo Lagao
Page ka profile photo lagana zaroori hai — yeh aapka pehla impression hai! Logo banane ke liye Canva.com use karo — bilkul free mein professional logo ban jaata hai. Size: 170x170 pixels best hota hai.
6
Cover Photo Lagao
Cover photo page ke upar dikhti hai — yeh bahut important hai! Canva mein "Facebook Cover" template dhundho — ready-made designs milenge. Size: 820x312 pixels. Apna page name aur topic clearly dikhao cover mein.
7
About Section Fill Karo
Page ka description likho — log yahan padhenge aapka page kis baare mein hai. 2-3 lines mein clearly batao. Website link bhi daalo agar hai. Email address bhi add karo contact ke liye.
8
Pehli Post Daalo
Page banane ke turant baad pehli post daalo! Introduction post likho — "Namaste dosto! Main hoon [naam] aur yeh page hai [topic] ke baare mein. Follow karo — roz helpful content milega!" Yeh post engaging honi chahiye!

📈 Facebook Page Par Followers Kaise Badhaye?

Page bana liya — ab followers badhane ka time! Yeh proven tarike use karo:

🔥 Followers Badhane Ke Top Tarike:

1. Roz Post Karo: Consistency sabse important hai — roz ek post minimum daalo.

2. Videos Daalo: Facebook par videos photos se 3x zyada reach milti hai!

3. Friends Ko Invite Karo: Page banane ke baad apne sab friends ko invite karo like karne ke liye.

4. Groups Mein Share Karo: Relevant Facebook groups mein apni posts share karo — lakhs log dekhenge!

5. Live Karo: Facebook Live se turant notification jaati hai — followers badhte hain!

6. Trending Topics Par Post Karo: Jo trending ho us par post karo — zyada reach milti hai!

⏰ Post Karne Ka Sahi Time

India mein Facebook use karne ka sabse zyada time yeh hai:

📅 Best Posting Times India:

🌅 Subah 7-9 Baje — Log office jaane se pehle check karte hain

🌞 Dopahar 1-2 Baje — Lunch break mein scrolling

🌆 Shaam 7-9 Baje — Sabse best time! Zyada users active

🌙 Raat 9-11 Baje — Students aur young users active

💡 Pro Tip: Shaam 8 baje India ka Facebook par sabse zyada traffic hota hai — yahi time post karo!

💰 Facebook Page Se Paise Kaise Kamaye?

💵 Paise Kamane Ke Tarike:

1. Facebook Ad Breaks: 10,000 followers aur 600,000 minutes views hone par videos par ads aa jaate hain — direct paise milte hain!

2. Sponsored Posts: Brands aapko apna product promote karne ke liye paisa dete hain — ₹2,000-20,000 per post!

3. Affiliate Marketing: Products ka link share karo — bikne par commission milta hai!

4. Apna Product Becho: Page se directly apna product ya service sell karo!

5. Facebook Stars: Live mein viewers aapko stars bhejte hain — yeh paise mein convert hote hain!
